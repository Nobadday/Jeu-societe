- The goal is to reach the end of the board.

- If during a move, you land on the same square as another player, you 
  challenge them to a duel. This event is not triggered if you land on
  the square via another event/effect.

- If you land on a chance square, you draw a chance card and must do
  what it says (bonus/penalty).

- When you land on a blocked area, you must roll the die and roll a 
  number equal to or greater than the value given.
